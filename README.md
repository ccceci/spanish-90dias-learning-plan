# 🇲🇽 Spanish 90 Days | 90天西班牙语学习计划

**A practical 90-day Spanish learning journey for everyday life and work in Mexico.**

一个面向西班牙语零基础学习者的 90 天学习项目，专注于墨西哥日常生活与职场沟通，从基础词汇逐步过渡到真实场景对话。

---

## 🎯 Learning Goals | 学习目标

- Build a foundation in Spanish pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar.
- Handle everyday conversations in Mexico.
- Communicate in basic workplace situations.
- Develop practical listening and speaking skills.
- Work toward an A2-level communication ability.

**目标：** 从 A0 基础开始，用 90 天建立实用西班牙语沟通能力，争取达到接近 A2 的日常交流水平。

## 📚 90-Day Learning Roadmap | 12周学习路线

| Week | Topic | 学习主题 |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Greetings & Introductions | 问候与自我介绍 |
| 02 | Numbers, Time & Dates | 数字、时间与日期 |
| 03 | Daily Routines & Verbs | 日常作息与动词 |
| 04 | Food & Ordering | 餐饮与点餐 |
| 05 | Shopping & Prices | 购物与价格 |
| 06 | Transportation & Directions | 交通与问路 |
| 07 | Home & Family | 住房与家庭 |
| 08 | Social Life & Invitations | 社交与邀约 |
| 09 | Workplace Communication | 职场沟通 |
| 10 | Past Events & Experiences | 过去事件与经历 |
| 11 | Health & Future Plans | 健康与未来计划 |
| 12 | A2 Review & Practice | 综合复习与实战 |

## ⏰ Daily Study Routine | 每日学习安排

**75–90 minutes per day · 6 study days + 1 review day**

- Vocabulary — 15 min
- Listening — 20 min
- Speaking — 20 min
- Grammar & Sentence Practice — 15 min
- Review — 5–20 min
